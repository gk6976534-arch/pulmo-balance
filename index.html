Pulmo Balance Ingredients

Pulmo Balance is presented as a daily wellness formula combining nutritional compounds and botanical extracts that are intended to support respiratory wellness, antioxidant protection, circulation, and healthy cellular energy. Magnesium Glycinate

Magnesium Glycinate provides magnesium in a form commonly used in nutritional supplements. Magnesium plays an important role in normal muscle and nerve function and contributes to numerous processes throughout the body. As part of a balanced diet, adequate magnesium intake can support normal muscular function and overall daily wellness.
2

Alpha-Lipoic Acid (ALA)

Alpha-Lipoic Acid is a naturally occurring antioxidant compound involved in cellular metabolism. It is included in many wellness formulas because of its role in helping the body manage oxidative stress. As part of Pulmo Balance, ALA is intended to complement the formula's broader antioxidant and cellular-support approach.
3

Butcher’s Broom Root Extract

Butcher’s Broom is a traditional botanical ingredient that has been used in herbal wellness preparations. Its inclusion in Pulmo Balance is intended to provide additional support for healthy circulation and normal vascular function. This makes it a complementary botanical component within the overall formula.

L-Carnitine

L-Carnitine is a naturally occurring compound involved in energy metabolism. It helps transport fatty acids within cells so they can be used in cellular energy production. By supporting normal energy metabolism, L-Carnitine can complement a daily wellness formula focused on maintaining energy and healthy cellular function.
5

Turmeric Extract

Turmeric Extract contains naturally occurring compounds called curcuminoids. Turmeric is widely used in traditional wellness practices and modern nutritional supplements for its antioxidant properties and support of a healthy inflammatory response. Within Pulmo Balance, it contributes botanical antioxidant support to the overall formulation.
6

Coenzyme Q10 (CoQ10)

Coenzyme Q10 is a naturally occurring compound found throughout the body and plays an important role in cellular energy production. It also has antioxidant properties. Its inclusion helps complement the formula's focus on cellular energy and protection from oxidative stress.
